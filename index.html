How enrolment works

  1. Share your qualification: Tell us whether you are in Class 10, Class 12 or hold a diploma / higher qualification.
  2. Discuss your preparation level: Identify the subjects and topics where you need support.
  3. Select a suitable duration: Discuss the 2, 4, 8 or 12-week format and class schedule.
  4. Begin preparation: Follow the agreed learning plan, practice schedule and assessments.
Program durations are proposed openEd Labs training formats, not official AME CET courses. Class frequency, delivery mode, fees, batch availability and start dates are confirmed individually before enrolment.
